Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

This policy covers Christmas TV: Yule Log & Films for Apple TV ("the app"). It explains what information the app uses, who handles it, and the choices you have.

No accounts

The app has no accounts. We don't ask for your name or email address, and we don't run servers that collect personal information about you.

What stays on your Apple TV

Some information is stored only on your device so the app works the way you left it:

  • Where you stopped in a film, so you can resume it
  • Your last yule log and music station, and your settings (music, fire sound, sleep timer, background motion)
  • Whether you've bought "Remove Ads"

We don't receive this information. Deleting the app removes it from your device. You can also clear your viewing history in Settings → Clear Continue Watching.

Advertising

The app is free and shows occasional video ads served by Google through the Google IMA SDK and Google Ad Manager. The app requests non-personalized ads only: it doesn't ask to track you and doesn't use your Apple TV's advertising identifier. To deliver an ad, Google may still process:

  • Your IP address, plus basic information about your device and the app
  • Ad interaction data, such as whether an ad was played, skipped or completed

Google uses this information to deliver ads, measure how they perform and prevent fraud, not to build an advertising profile of you from this app. To learn more, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

In-app purchase

"Remove Ads" is a one-time purchase of $2.99. Apple processes the payment, and we never see your payment details. The app only learns whether the purchase went through.

Content delivery

The app downloads its catalog and streams video and music from a hosting / content delivery provider. Like most web servers, that provider may log IP addresses as part of normal operation.

Children

Christmas TV is made for the whole family. Ads in the app are non-personalized, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
