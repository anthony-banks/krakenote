Frequently asked

How do I change the music on a yule log?

While a yule log is playing, press the remote to show its controls. You can switch the music station, turn the fire sound on or off, or skip the song. To turn the music or the fire sound off for every yule log, use Settings → Channels.

Is there a sleep timer?

Yes. Press the remote while a yule log is playing and choose a sleep timer. When it runs out, the picture and sound fade out gently.

Why do some films have Christmas music instead of their own sound?

Silent films and some early cartoons have no soundtrack, so the app plays Christmas music over them. You can turn it off from the music button while watching, or in Settings → Silent Films.

A film or yule log won't play.

Everything streams over the internet, so first check your Apple TV's connection. Christmas TV needs tvOS 18 or later. If a title still won't play, email us its name and we'll take a look.

Where do the films come from?

The movies and cartoons are in the public domain in the United States. Credits for every film, recording and music track are in Settings → About → Credits. If you believe a title shouldn't be in the app, please email us and we'll review it promptly.
