Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

This policy covers two Apple TV apps: Midnight Vault and Midnight Vault Trivia (together, "the apps"). It explains what information the apps use, who handles it, and the choices you have.

No accounts

The apps don't have accounts. We don't ask for your name or email address in the apps, and we don't run our own servers that collect personal information about you.

What stays on your Apple TV

Some information is stored only on your device so the apps work the way you left them:

  • Game stats in Midnight Vault Trivia: scores, streaks and your best round
  • Your sound on/off setting

We don't receive this information. Deleting an app removes it from your device.

No ads, no purchases

Since version 1.0.1, neither app shows ads, includes advertising or tracking code, or has in-app purchases. Earlier versions showed video ads served by Google and offered a "Remove Ads" purchase; updating to the latest version removes both.

Game Center (Midnight Vault Trivia)

Leaderboards are run by Apple's Game Center. If you're signed in, other players can see your Game Center nickname and scores. Apple handles this data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Content delivery

The apps download question and catalog files, and Midnight Vault streams video, from a hosting / content delivery provider. Like most web servers, that provider may log IP addresses as part of normal operation.

Children

The apps contain horror themes and aren't directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date above.
