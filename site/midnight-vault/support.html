Frequently asked

How do I remove ads?

Midnight Vault Trivia has a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase. Go to Home → Remove Ads.

I bought Remove Ads on another Apple TV, or reinstalled the app.

Go to Home → Remove Ads and choose Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

Leaderboards aren't showing.

Leaderboards need Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your Apple TV's Settings, then reopen the app.

Are the films really free?

Yes. Midnight Vault streams public-domain films, and ads pay for it.

I'm an indie filmmaker. Can my film be on Midnight Vault?

We'd love to hear about your horror or sci-fi film. Email midnightvault666@gmail.com. Deals are non-exclusive, and you get a share of the ad revenue.
