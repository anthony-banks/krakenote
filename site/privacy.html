Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Krakenote ("Krakenote," "we," "us") collects, uses and shares information when you use the Krakenote iOS app, the web app at krakenote.com, and this website (together, the "Service").

Information we collect

  • Account information: your email address and, if you provide it, your first and last name. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive the email address (or Apple's private relay address) and name that you choose to share.
  • Your content: notes, notebooks, decks and flashcards you create, and the material you give us to make cards from, such as pasted text, photos, PDFs and other documents, and web or video links.
  • Study activity: your reviews and grades, which we use for spaced-repetition scheduling, streaks and progress stats.
  • Purchases: whether you have a Pro subscription and its status and renewal date. Payments are handled by Apple (on iOS) or our payment processor (on the web). We never see or store your full card details.
  • Usage events: basic in-app events (for example, "deck created" or "AI generation used"), tied to your account, that help us understand which features are used. These events don't include the content of your notes or cards.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (such as IP address, device and request time), used for security, abuse prevention and keeping the Service running.
  • Feedback: anything you send us through the in-app feedback form or by email.

We don't use advertising trackers, we don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not sell or rent your personal information.

How we use your information

  • To provide the Service: syncing your notes and decks across devices, scheduling reviews, and generating flashcards and other AI results you ask for.
  • To manage your account and subscription, and enforce plan limits such as the daily AI allowance.
  • To keep the Service secure and prevent abuse.
  • To understand usage and improve Krakenote.
  • To respond to your support requests and feedback, and send essential account emails (such as sign-in verification).

AI processing

When you use an AI feature (generating cards, fact-checking a card or cleaning up a note), the content you choose is sent from our servers to Anthropic, our AI provider, to produce the result. The app asks for your permission before the first time this happens, and you can turn AI data sharing off at any time under Account → AI data sharing. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic does not use this content to train its models, and it keeps it only briefly for safety and abuse monitoring. We don't use your content to train AI models either.

Service providers we share with

We share information only with providers that help us run the Service, and only as much as they need to do so:

  • Supabase: database, file storage and sign-in.
  • Railway: application hosting.
  • Anthropic: AI processing (see above).
  • RevenueCat: subscription management, linked to your account ID.
  • Apple and Google: sign-in, if you choose those options; Apple also processes App Store payments.
  • Our web payment processor: handles payments made on the web.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users or Krakenote.

Shared notes

If you create a share link for a note, anyone with that link can view the note. You can turn the link off at any time from the note's share menu.

Retention and deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Account → Delete account) or on the web. This permanently deletes your account, notes, decks, cards, saved sources and study history. Feedback you've sent is kept without your email address. Server logs and backups expire on their normal schedule. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel it in your Apple ID settings first.

Your rights and choices

  • You can view and edit your content in the app, export decks as CSV on the web, and update your name under Account.
  • You can turn AI data sharing off under Account → AI data sharing.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your personal information, or object to how we process it. To exercise these rights, email support@krakenote.com.

Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS) and access controls that limit each account to its own data. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and fix issues quickly.

Children's privacy

Krakenote is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

International users

Krakenote is operated from the United States, and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Krakenote evolves. If we make material changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, let you know in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests? Reach us at support@krakenote.com.
