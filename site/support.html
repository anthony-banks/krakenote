Frequently asked

What is Krakenote?

An AI-first study app. Take notes in clean notebooks, then turn any note, photo, PDF, slide deck, or web article into instant flashcards, quizzes, and an AI tutor — with a spaced-repetition review loop that drills you on exactly what you're about to forget.

Is Krakenote free?

Yes — there's a free plan to get started with a daily AI allowance. Krakenote Pro unlocks unlimited, smarter AI, the tutor, and exam mode for $5.99/month or $59.99/year (about two months free), with a 7-day free trial.

What can I turn into flashcards and quizzes?

Typed notes, photos of handwritten notes, PDFs, Word (.docx) and PowerPoint (.pptx) files, web articles, and YouTube videos. Krakenote reads the material and generates study cards and quizzes from it.

Does it work on iPhone and the web?

The full web app is live now at krakenote.com/app. A native iOS app is coming soon, and everything syncs across your devices.

How does the review system work?

Krakenote uses spaced repetition: it schedules each card so you review it right before you'd forget it, which is the most efficient way to move facts into long-term memory.

How do I cancel Pro?

Open Account → Billing and choose Manage subscription — you can cancel anytime, and Pro stays active through the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I delete my account or data?

Email support@krakenote.com and we'll remove it promptly. See our Privacy Policy for details.
