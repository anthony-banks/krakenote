Frequently asked

Does Krakenote use spaced repetition like Anki?

Yes — it schedules each card so you review it right before you'd forget it, the same memory-science idea Anki uses.

Is Krakenote free like Anki?

Krakenote has a free plan plus a free no-signup tool. Anki is free on desktop/Android but a one-time paid app on iOS. Krakenote Pro is $5.99/month.

Can it make cards from my lecture PDF?

Yes — upload a PDF, slides, a photo, or paste text and it generates the flashcards and a summary.
