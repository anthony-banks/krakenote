# Chemistry basics — test notes

Study material for testing formula rendering (KaTeX) and cloze cards.

## Common molecules
- Water is H2O.
- Carbon dioxide is CO2.
- Glucose is C6H12O6.

## The mole
Avogadro's number is approximately 6.022 x 10^23 particles per mole.

## Ideal gas law
The ideal gas law relates pressure, volume, moles, and temperature: P V = n R T,
where n is the number of moles and R is the universal gas constant.

## pH
pH is the negative base-10 logarithm of the hydrogen ion concentration:
pH = -log[H+]. A neutral solution has pH = 7.

## Combustion of methane
Methane burns in oxygen to give carbon dioxide and water:
CH4 + 2 O2 -> CO2 + 2 H2O.
