## Equations of motion (constant acceleration)
- Final velocity: v = u + a t
- Displacement: s = u t + (1/2) a t^2
- Velocity–displacement relation: v^2 = u^2 + 2 a s

Here u is initial velocity, v is final velocity, a is acceleration, t is time,
and s is displacement.

## Newton's laws
- Second law: force equals mass times acceleration, F = m a.
- Momentum: p = m v.

## Energy
- Kinetic energy: KE = (1/2) m v^2
- Gravitational potential energy near Earth's surface: PE = m g h
- Mass–energy equivalence: E = m c^2

## Circular motion
Centripetal acceleration points toward the center of the circle: a_c = v^2 / r.

## The quadratic formula (used to solve for time in projectile problems)
For a x^2 + b x + c = 0, the solutions are x = (-b ± sqrt(b^2 - 4 a c)) / (2 a).
